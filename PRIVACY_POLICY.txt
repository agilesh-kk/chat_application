PRIVACY POLICY
Memento Chat Application
Last Updated: August 17, 2026

INTRODUCTION

This Privacy Policy describes how Memento collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application 'Memento'. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about the data we collect and how it is used.


INFORMATION WE COLLECT

1. Account Information
When you create an account, we collect:
- Full name
- Email address
- Password (securely handled by Firebase Authentication)
- Date of birth
- Gender
- Profile picture (preset or custom uploaded)
- Bio (optional)

2. User-Generated Content
The App collects content you create and share, including:
- Text messages sent in conversations
- Image messages sent in conversations
- Status updates (images and captions)
- Timeline events and memories
- Watch2gether room content (messages, images, video queue)
- Message reactions and replies

3. Usage and Device Information
We automatically collect:
- Online status and last seen timestamps
- Typing indicators and in-chat presence
- Device/platform information
- Push notification tokens (for delivering notifications)

4. Locally Stored Data
The App stores data on your device for performance and offline access:
- Cached messages and conversation history (SQLite database)
- Cached images (profile pictures, chat images, status images)
- User session data (SharedPreferences)
- Message drafts
- Notification preferences


HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

1. To provide and maintain the App's core functionality, including messaging,
   friend connections, status updates, and Watch2gether features.

2. To authenticate your account and verify your identity.

3. To deliver push notifications for new messages, friend requests, and
   other App activities.

4. To enable real-time features such as typing indicators and online
   presence status.

5. To process and deliver scheduled messages (Time Capsule feature).

6. To store and serve media content you upload (images, status updates).

7. To analyze App usage and improve our services through Firebase Analytics.

8. To ensure the security and integrity of our platform.


DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

1. Cloud Storage
Your data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure provided by:
- Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore Database, Realtime Database,
  Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging)
- Supabase (PostgreSQL Database, Object Storage for media files)

2. Local Storage
Certain data is cached on your device using:
- SQLite database (messages, conversations)
- Hive database (status data, profile metadata)
- SharedPreferences (session data, preferences)
- File system (cached images)

3. Security Measures
- Passwords are encrypted and never stored in plain text
- We use industry-standard encryption for data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
- Firebase and Supabase provide built-in security and encryption
- Access to your data is restricted to authorized systems only

4. Data Retention
- Account data is retained as long as your account is active
- Messages and content are retained until you delete them or your account
- Locally cached data is stored on your device and can be cleared by
  uninstalling the App or clearing App data


DATA SHARING

1. With Other Users
Certain information is visible to other users of the App:
- Your name and profile picture
- Your online status and last seen timestamp
- Messages you send in conversations
- Status updates you post (visible to friends)
- Watch2gether room participation

2. With Service Providers
We share data with third-party service providers who assist in operating
the App:
- Google Firebase: Authentication, database hosting, cloud functions,
  push notifications
- Supabase: Secondary database hosting and media file storage

These service providers are contractually obligated to protect your data
and use it only for the purposes of providing services to us.

3. We Do Not Sell Your Data
We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.
We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to
valid requests by public authorities.


YOUR RIGHTS

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

1. Access: You can view your personal information and data within the App.

2. Correction: You can update your profile information at any time through
   the App's profile settings.

3. Deletion: You can request deletion of your account and associated data
   by contacting us at the email address below.

4. Data Portability: You can request a copy of your data in a commonly
   used format by contacting us.

5. Opt-Out: You can disable push notifications through your device settings.

6. Local Data: You can clear locally cached data by clearing the App's
   data in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.


CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or the
applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly
collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware
that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will
take steps to delete such information promptly.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with
personal information, please contact us immediately.


PERMISSIONS

The App requests the following device permissions:

1. Camera: To take photos for chat messages, profile pictures, and status
   updates.

2. Photos/Media: To access your device gallery for selecting images to
   share in conversations and as profile pictures.

3. Notifications: To send you push notifications for new messages, friend
   requests, and other App activities.

4. Battery Optimization: To ensure reliable delivery of push notifications
   even when your device is in battery saver mode.
